# Red Hat advisories

Original publisher: Red Hat Product Security.

`rhsa.rss` is the current advisory feed. The complete downloaded weekly CSAF
advisory and VEX archives are retained locally under `archives/`. Their original
SHA-256 files, signatures and update indexes are under `archive-metadata/`.
Weekly archives reflect their filenames' dates; subsequent changes require the
published update indexes. Downloaded signatures are preserved; signature
authenticity has not been independently verified.

Official bulk data: https://security.access.redhat.com/data/csaf/v2/

The large compressed archives are excluded from ordinary Git commits. Their
embedded notices and the original Red Hat terms continue to apply.
