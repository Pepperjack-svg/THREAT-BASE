# Source provenance

The [source register](../DATA_SOURCES.md) names the original publishers and their terms.
CVE records come directly from CVEProject/cvelistV5. The automated NVD tree follows
[fkie-cad/nvd-json-data-feeds](https://github.com/fkie-cad/nvd-json-data-feeds), a community
reconstruction of NIST NVD; local yearly archives are downloaded directly from NIST.

THREAT-BASE retains upstream records rather than adding normalized projections.
GitHub, PyPA, MITRE and CISA repository data keep their upstream notices. Full OSV,
EPSS history and Red Hat CSAF/VEX archives remain local downloads until selected
for distribution under their applicable terms. CERT/CC remains excluded pending
redistribution review. A downloaded snapshot is not full Git revision history.
